Swachta is a waste-management platform that turns every pickup and every complaint into a verified, tracked ticket — so households know their waste was collected, collectors get a fair earnings record, and cities get data they can act on.

See the pilot plan Talk to us
The problem

City waste is collected every day. Almost none of it is verified.

Pickups happen — or don't — with no record. Complaints disappear into registers with no deadline and no owner. Segregation at home is asked for but never measured, so recyclable material arrives mixed and worthless, and the collectors who move a city's waste have no proof of the work they did.

No verification

A missed pickup and a completed one look identical on paper. Households pay, societies guess, municipalities fly blind.

No traceability

Plastic and other recyclables are never weighed or traced, so good material is priced like mixed waste — and most of it is landfilled instead.

No record for collectors

Informal collectors carry the system, yet have no verified earnings history to show a bank, a landlord, or a contractor.

How Swachta works

One platform, three apps, every pickup a ticket.

1

Requestor App

For households and societies: scheduled pickups by waste stream (wet, dry, sanitary, special care), photo-based issue reporting with location, payments, and live ticket tracking.

2

Collector App

For collectors: the day's route, pickup confirmation by photo and weight, and a verified earnings ledger. Works offline and syncs when back in coverage.

3

Authority Console

For municipal officers: a hotspot map built from citizen reports, auto-routed tickets with SLA timers and escalation, and ward-level compliance reports.

The platform is configuration-driven: each city and ward is a settings profile — wards, tariffs, SLA rules, languages — not a new codebase. What works in one Ghaziabad ward can be switched on in the next city.

Circularity

The plastic loop: from household bin to a lot a recycler can price.

Plastic loses its value at the first mile — mixed, dirty and unweighed. Swachta fixes the first mile, so segregated plastic reaches recyclers as graded, traceable lots instead of landfill.

1 · Segregate

Households sort at source into four streams, with a segregation score per home and society.

2 · Collect & weigh

Every pickup is photo-confirmed and weighed in the Collector App — the weight is the record.

3 · Sort & bale

Dry plastic is sorted by polymer at a ward site and baled (flaked where clean enough to earn more).

4 · Trace

Each lot carries its source, weight, polymer and date — a chain of custody buyers and cities can audit.

5 · Sell & pay fairly

Graded lots are sold to aggregators and recyclers; collectors are paid against verified weights.
